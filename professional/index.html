The Work

Most contact centres are held together with duct tape. Most hospital systems run on integrations that were never designed to talk to each other. I fix both.

Twenty years building from the ground up — IT support, desktop, VoIP engineering at one of Australia's largest regional banks, network infrastructure, and now architecture. Every layer is another view of what breaks under pressure and why.

The difference isn't knowing the technology. It's owning the whole environment. Most vendors deliver one piece and walk. In healthcare, that's not good enough — when one part breaks, it's a patient problem, not a tech problem.
