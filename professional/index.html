AI Practice

I've never built an AI system to cut headcount. I've built them so the people left have harder, more interesting problems to solve.

Most AI projects get deployed and then handed to whoever wrote the original workflows. The technology changes. The process around it doesn't. That's where it falls apart. Every system I build is designed around what the human needs to do differently — not just what the AI can now handle.

AI Practice Lead, in practice, means I talk to the people using the system before I spec anything. A contact centre team knows things about why calls fail that no vendor has ever asked them. A clinical team can tell you exactly where the information gap is costing them time. I start there.

Amplify

Get the right information to the right person before they have to ask for it.

Automate

The calls that don't need a human in the loop — handle them. Free up the ones who do.

Augment

The agent with AI assist handles things that used to need a supervisor. Same person, higher ceiling.
