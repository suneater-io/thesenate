Architect

The role fits the way I think.

20+ years in IT. The title caught up eventually. It started with a 486 in my parents' house — I pulled everything apart to see how it worked. Never stopped. Systems, structure, how things connect — that's not work. That's just how I see everything. Rules are starting points. You learn them until you know which ones to cross.

02 / Detroit

Detroit

Not where I'm from. Where I belong.

It started with the Pistons. Everyone liked the Bulls. I wanted the team that made Jordan's life miserable — the team that refused to back down. That was Detroit. The Wings came next. Blue collar to the bone. Hard work rewarded. Knocked down, keeps showing up. That mentality doesn't just describe a city. It describes how I want to operate.

03 / Metal

Metal

Not a taste. A life support system.

Music is not something I do. It's what I am. Meshuggah at 14 changed everything — not just the music, the idea that something could break every rule and be better for it. Like the music was demonstrating the way my brain worked. Chaotic, loud, odd, different. Thirty years in. Still my medicine. Still my brain. Without it, nothing else around it works. Nothing else exists.
